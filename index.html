Executive Summary

Robert Allen is a product executive, entrepreneur, and independent researcher based in Sebastian, Florida. He retired at 36 and now directs his energy toward building companies, advancing scientific research, and giving back to the institutions that shaped him.

Robert's career spans two decades of high-impact product leadership. As a product executive at Amazon and Discover Financial Services, he delivered results measured in tens of millions of dollars — reducing customer acquisition costs by 42% at Amazon and generating $43M in impact at Discover in under 18 months. He has led international cross-functional teams across 13 countries, built enterprise product organizations from scratch, and managed budgets and stakeholder relationships at the highest levels.

Today, Robert's professional focus spans investment, technology, and science. As founder of ZingLab, an independent research laboratory, he developed an early-onset dementia risk assessment tool in collaboration with researchers at Loughborough University — a simple, accessible digital test now informing new lines of cognitive decline research. ZingLab is currently conducting original experiments in microbiome science, exploring targeted interventions with implications for human health. He also holds a utility patent and is bringing his Nested Hex Adapter device to market through major online retail channels.

A published author of four science fiction novels, Robert brings creative range to everything he does. He has served as a guest speaker in AI debates and is an active voice in the technology community. He currently serves as a Trustee on the Sebastian Police Pension Board and as Marketing Committee Chair for Florida Tech's College of Business — his alma mater, where he earned his B.S. in International Economics. He later completed his MBA in Management Information Systems at Rochester Institute of Technology.
